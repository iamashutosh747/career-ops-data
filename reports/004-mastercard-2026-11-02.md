Title And Summary

Senior Analyst -Financial Planning & Analysis - Automation

Mastercard is a global technology company in the payments industry. Our mission is to connect and power an inclusive, digital economy that benefits everyone, everywhere by making transactions safe, simple, smart, and accessible. Using secure data and networks, partnerships and passion, our innovations and solutions help individuals, financial institutions, governments, and businesses realize their greatest potential.

Our decency quotient, or DQ, drives our culture and everything we do inside and outside of our company. With connections across more than 210 countries and territories, we are building a sustainable world that unlocks priceless possibilities for all.

Senior Analyst, FP&A, will assist in driving standardization of reporting across all regions, leveraging tools like Power BI, Microsoft Fabric, SQL, advanced Excel. This position requires a highly analytical, independent thinker who possesses strong communication skills & strategic thinking that is highly motivated. The position will also provide exposure to various levels of management as well as cross-functional partnerships.

Key Responsibilities

Building machine learning solutions to generate actionable insights.

Build backend structures/data bases structure for dashboards.

Developing and Maintaining centralized Dashboards and MIS

Collaborate with Americas & Corporate FP&A to develop and maintain advanced analytics and data science tools

Train team on automation skills and extend support to workstreams

Qualifications

Education & Experience:

Strong academic background in Computer Science, Data Science, Technology, or a related field

Experience in data management, mining, analytics, reporting & quantitative analysis.

Technical Skills

Proficiency in Python, Power BI, Fabric and SQL.

Familiarity with machine learning frameworks, libraries, data structures, and software architecture.

Experience with Big projects on Tableau or Power BI

Self-motivated with a proven track record of delivering success while operating in a team environment.

Analytical & Communication Skills

Strong analytical skills with a proven ability to translate data into compelling insights.

Experience interacting with stakeholders to understand technical needs and deliver effective solutions.
