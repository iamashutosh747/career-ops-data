Job Description

Key Responsibilities

- Analyse large volumes of financial and operational data to provide actionable insights.
- Translate non-financial data and key performance indicators (KPIs) into financial metrics and assess their impact on financial models.
- Produce high-quality financial reports tailored to internal stakeholders, with a particular focus on departmental performance.
- Conduct cost analysis and optimization, identifying opportunities to enhance profitability and efficiency.
- Support forecasting, budgeting, variance analysis, and financial planning processes.
- Track key financial and operational KPIs, monitoring cost trends and assessing their financial impact.
- Partner with various business units to provide financial insights and recommendations that drive strategic decision-making.
- Collaborate closely with the Group Services CFO, assisting in financial reporting and analysis for global executive leadership.
- Work in a fast-paced, agile environment, adapting to evolving business needs and priorities.

Key Requirements

Experience: 4-8 years of relevant experience in Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A), Finance Analytics, Management Reporting, or a similar finance analyst role with strong exposure to data-driven decision-making

- Proven experience as a Finance Analyst/Senior Finance Analyst, or in a similar role with a strong focus on data analysis and reporting.
- Expertise in handling and analysing large financial datasets, drawing meaningful insights.
- Strong ability to convert non-financial data and KPIs into financial metrics and assess their business impact.
- Experience producing departmental financial reports for internal stakeholders, ensuring accuracy and clarity.
- Advanced proficiency in financial modelling, forecasting, and planning.
- Solid understanding of cost analysis, cost optimization, and profitability improvement.
- Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills with a keen eye for detail.
- Ability to work independently in a fast-paced environment while managing multiple priorities.
- Strong business acumen with the ability to partner effectively with operational teams and senior stakeholders.
- Proficiency in financial software, Excel (advanced), and BI tools (e.g., Power BI, Tableau, or similar).
- Strong communication skills with the ability to present financial data clearly to both financial and non-financial audiences.

If you are an experienced financial analyst with a passion for data-driven decision-making, cost optimization, and financial reporting, we would love to hear from you!
